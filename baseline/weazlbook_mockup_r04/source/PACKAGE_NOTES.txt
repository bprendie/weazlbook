WEAZLBOOK R04 — DIMENSIONED APPROVAL BLOCKOUT

Purpose: review the appearance and proposed packing of an 80 x 156 mm reader
with a nominal 14 mm top and 26 mm lower grip. This is not production CAD,
not a firmware release, and not a print plate. The user has reserved the CAD,
print-plate and software handoff for after approval of this mockup.

build_mockup.py constructs the same CadQuery solids for all six rendered
views. It uses millimetres, with X right, Y down and Z rearward from the front.
No generative image is used to establish mechanical fit. The sample reading
text is original placeholder prose, set in IBM Plex Mono.

component_layout.json separates published dimensions, photo-derived locations
and design allowances. Blue/grey/green blocks are component envelopes, not
invented exact models of vendor hardware. Amber blocks represent provisional
FPC fold space and the PH battery plug/lead fit check. Colored harness lines
show routing corridors, not a final wire count or electrical pinout.

The selected display is the current Good Display GDEY0397T81P plus its
manufacturer-supported DESPI-C02 adapter. The supplier's purchase page still
uses a legacy GDEM name in its URL/title. Verify the delivered current suffix
against the drawing before releasing production mounting features.

Unresolved local measurements: actual nav mechanism/stem/cap interface;
complete driver height and seated FPC position; mainboard assembly heights;
simultaneous USB cable boot and mated battery plug clearance; the protected
cell and lead exit. These are disclosed in the approval PDF. Battery runtime
and radio performance have not been measured. The 2–3 day reading goal is
a future firmware/power validation target, not a result of this model.

Reproduction requires Python 3, CadQuery 2.7, NumPy, Pillow, ModernGL with a
headless EGL context, and ReportLab. The original environment used llvmpipe.
Keep the archived research/fonts directory next to weazlbook_mockup_r04.
IBM Plex Mono is bundled with its SIL Open Font License.

Run source/build_mockup.py, source/compose_overview.py and then
source/build_approval_pdf.py from the extracted project. The original runtime
made ModernGL available with PYTHONPATH=/tmp/weazlbook-render-deps; a normal
Python environment with ModernGL installed does not need that setting.

Design cues: acid-green shell, black service cover and bezel, exposed screws,
faceted edges, cyan/magenta printed inserts, a black rubber thumb nub, two
lower keys, and the quiet NEVER TAX THE GIG inscription inside the cover.
