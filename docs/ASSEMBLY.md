# R05 assembly sequence and unresolved retention

All STEP/STL parts are in millimetres. Coordinates are R04 front view: X right,
Y down, Z rearward. Main exterior envelope remains 80 × 156 mm, nominal rear
plane Z=14+Y/13. Do not scale hardware to fix a fit; ABS shrink correction is
currently 1.0 until coupon measurements establish a value.

The exported model is an unprinted engineering prototype. Zero intersection
volume against conservative blocks is necessary but does not validate connector
geometry, tolerance, strength, fastener installation or service access.

1. Print/check screw, key, cap and connector coupons first. Complete the register.
2. Deburr the shell. Test M2 service screws in pilot coupons. The provisional
   1.7 mm pilot has 7 mm depth; cover head recess and screw length must leave
   positive floor thickness. M2 × 8 is the initial service-screw candidate.
3. Dry-fit bezel and display with the R04 56.84 × 97.22 mm relief at Z2.1. Add a
   measured compliant perimeter gasket without pressure points on the glass.
   Bezel is recessed into a real pocket and held by four M1.6 candidates; these
   short pilots need coupon/pullout validation before repeated servicing.
4. Install display bridges over the reserved rear insulation. They have 0.1 mm
   nominal separation from its envelope. Confirm pads actually support the panel
   without clamping or point loads. The lower bridge is partial to clear battery.
5. Test nav carrier on its front/chin columns. Its fastening holes are **carrier
   holes**, not invented navigation-PCB holes. The real PCB rests at Z9.4. Two
   left-edge retainers sit above the conservative rear envelope; select insulating
   shims after confirming component-free PCB bearing areas. Right-side support
   screws install before the PCB. Thumb force goes into carrier/columns/chin,
   never glass, FPC or battery. Prove lateral retention under repeated thumb load.
6. Stretch the replaceable95A TPU shoes over the rigid ABS key heads; their
   retaining lips capture the heads without adhesives. Test the0.1 mm clearance,
   pad compliance and retention on the key coupon. Assemble BACK/MARK from inside
   before switch carriers. Faces have0.2 mm
   per-side guide clearance; flange and stop surfaces are separate from switch
   travel. Initial no-load plunger gap is 0.2 mm, mechanical stop travel 0.4 mm.
   **Switch-carrier retention is not resolved.** The 12 × 10 mm bays do not leave
   room for an assumed side snap. Do not glue them into a supposedly serviceable
   final assembly; measure the switch installation and design a removable chin
   rail before the enclosure release. This is a CAD gate, not a finished joint.
7. Install mainboard on the preserved R04 underlap supports at Z14. Three
   removable edge clamps use support pilots outside the PCB, without adding board
   holes. Two antenna-end board holes exist in the official drawing but are not
   used in this prototype. Verify clamps touch only component-free board edges;
   retain native boot/reset and rear card access.
8. Fit both USB cable and factory PH battery plug. The local coupon preserves
   the open recess, rather than replacing it with a narrow tunnel. Measure the
   lead turn; do not bend factory protected-cell leads against the wall.
9. Install the battery tray on two shell-side lugs. R05 lowers its rim by 0.5 mm
   to improve rear clearance, retaining the R04 bay and lead notch. Use a loose
   nonconductive strap through the side slots with a pull tab; no pouch compression.
   Tape, protection board and lead exit must fit the measured envelope.
10. Driver carrier has real side cheeks and Y slots, ±3 mm adjustment. It mounts
    to cover-side bosses; the stock header has a cut relief. Determine exact
    component-free bearing surfaces and axial PCB restraint from the delivered
    adapter. Carrier adjustment must be checked against the whole flex topology,
    not just the nominal tip. It is not permission to bend the real FPC at R2.
11. Route the eight display wires through the X33–35 corridor and the controls
    behind the chin. Provide lacing/strain relief that does not pinch flex or cell.
    The proposed VBUS sensing circuit still needs its own insulated mounting
    detail, listed in the register; its parts are not silently omitted from BOM.
12. With hardware unplugged, verify rear cover seats without resistance; inspect
    all points with witness material/feeler gauges. Check screw lengths and torque.
    Only then perform electrical bring-up. Separate color inserts fit their
    recessed pockets; use removable adhesive film if their press fit fails.

The driver is attached to the cover, so opening requires controlled movement and
flex access. A fully proven service sequence remains a physical gate. Do not use
the cover as a lever to extract its FPC. Marked “NEVER TAX THE GIG” engraving is on
the inside, not the exterior.

No drop, squeeze, fatigue, battery temperature, RF, card extraction or print-fit
results exist yet. Record actual results rather than converting these instructions
into assumed passes.
